Add `exactly(n, p)` returning `Parser([]T)` — match `p` exactly `n` times (comptime `n`). Disambiguates from `many` (zero+) and `manyOne` (one+) — the bound is exact, not min/max. Pre-allocates `n * @sizeOf(T)` bytes once (single alloc, no growth) — cheaper than `many`'s growable `ArrayList` when the count is known ahead of time. On err, the partial buffer is freed before propagating; the inner parser's err passes through unchanged with the cursor at the failing position. `n = 0` is a trivial success with an empty slice and the cursor unchanged.
