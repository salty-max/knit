Perf: char-family parsers (`char`, `satisfy`, `oneOf`, `noneOf`) now borrow `ParseError.actual` directly from `state.input` instead of allocating a UTF-8 encoding via the state allocator. Zero allocation on err. Same `?[]const u8` content visible to consumers; the borrowed slice's lifetime is the caller's input (strictly more durable than the previous arena-bound allocation). For grammars heavy in `choice` / `possibly` / `lookAhead` (which discard the inner err), this eliminates per-attempt churn — e.g. `choice` of 5 char alternatives at one cursor now allocates 0 bytes instead of 5.

**Breaking (internal-but-public):** `core.encodeCpAlloc` is removed — no longer needed. Anyone who happened to call it externally needs to inline its body or use `std.unicode.utf8Encode` directly. Closes #97.
