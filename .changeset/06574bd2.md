Add `startOfInput()` returning `Parser(void)` — assert the cursor is at index 0. Symmetric to `endOfInput`. O(1) check, zero allocation; the err carries no `actual` slice (the violating position is captured in `err.index` and the leftover IS the entire input — no preview to add). Useful inside `lookAhead`, `chain`, or composed grammars where a child needs to verify it ran before any consumption (e.g. shebang-like top-level directives).
