**Breaking:** remove `parsil.label`. Use `parsil.tag(p, name)` instead — same semantics (replaces `err.parser`), inferred-type API, harmonized argument order. The two parsers shipped together briefly (PR #109) with a documented "will converge" note; v1.0 cuts the duplicate ahead of release. Pair with `parsil.expect(p, msg)` if you also want to replace `err.message`. Migration: `label([]const u8, "name", p)` → `tag(p, "name")`.
