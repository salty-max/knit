Add `many(T, p)` and `manyOne(T, p)` repetition combinators under `parsers/many/`. `many` is zero-or-more (always succeeds); `manyOne` requires at least one match and propagates the inner parser's error verbatim on miss. Both collect into a `[]T` slice allocated via `state.allocator` and use the canonical arena-per-parse pattern. Both stop on a no-progress iteration (inner succeeded without consuming input) — guards against infinite loops with parsers like `succeed`, `lookahead`, or zero-or-more inner combinators. Mirrors the parsil-TS issue #26 fix. Shared collection logic lives in a colocated `internal.zig`.
