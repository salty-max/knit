**Breaking:** rename `parsil.bit.bit()` to `parsil.bit.any()`. The
old name awkwardly stuttered the namespace (`P.bit.bit()`) and gave
no signal that it's the value-agnostic single-bit reader. The new
name mirrors `anyChar()`'s "single, any value" semantics and reads
naturally next to its sibling assertions `bit.zero()` / `bit.one()`.
The `ParseError.parser` identity also moves from `"bit"` to
`"bit.any"` for consistency with the rest of the bit family.
Migration: `parsil.bit.bit()` → `parsil.bit.any()`.
